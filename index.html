Understanding the numbers

The Air Quality Index (AQI) summarizes how polluted the air is. PM2.5 measures fine particles, while CO₂ is reported in parts per million (ppm). Compare measurements over time and across locations to spot patterns.

0–50 Good
51–100 Moderate
101+ Unhealthy
